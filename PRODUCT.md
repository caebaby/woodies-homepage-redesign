# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Drivers across North and South Carolina who need maintenance, diagnostics, tires, or urgent repair. They often arrive worried, short on time, and uncertain whom to trust.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors quickly choose a nearby Woodie's shop, understand why it is trustworthy, and either call or schedule service with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Established, neighborly, capable. The experience should reduce anxiety and feel like a dependable local shop with modern service standards.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic national-chain polish, page-builder carousels, aspirational road-trip advertising, oversized mobile typography, vague trust claims, and long brochure pages that separate proof from action.

## Design Principles

1. Reassure before asking for commitment.
2. Make the nearest real person easy to call.
3. Show proof where decisions happen.
4. Use heritage as evidence, not decoration.
5. Keep every screen focused on the next useful action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, support keyboard navigation and reduced motion, retain visible focus states, use plain language, and keep primary touch targets at least 44 pixels.
